Hello and welcome! I’m so glad you stopped by this quiet corner of my site.

If you’re wondering about the name, it comes down to how I approach learning. Beyond the obvious nickname pun, I love the process of sifting through papers, codebases, and textbooks to handpick the best data science takeaways, AI research, and creative ideas I come across, then bringing them together in one place.

As a first-year Computer Science student at Georgia State University focusing on Data Science, Machine Learning, and Artificial Intelligence, I wanted a space that reflects both my technical side and my actual lived experience.

The reality is that navigating college as a CS major on an H4 visa comes with its own unique set of unspoken hurdles, waiting games, and structural constraints. I started this notebook to document all of it openly: from late-night debugging victories to visa hurdles, coursework reflections, and the quiet milestones in between. My hope is that being honest about the journey serves as a helpful, reassuring guide for anyone walking a similar path.
